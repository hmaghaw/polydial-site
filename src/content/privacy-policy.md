# PolyDial Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: 2025-10-07**

This Privacy Policy explains how PolyDial, operated by IdeationMax Inc. ("we," "us," or "our"), collects, uses, and shares information when merchants and their customers use our AI-powered phone ordering and management platform ("PolyDial Platform" or "Services"). This policy applies to users in the **United States and Canada**.

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Merchant Information (from Integrated Platforms)

When a merchant connects PolyDial to their Point of Sale (POS) system or business management platform (such as Clover, Square, Toast, or other supported platforms), we collect and process limited information from their account, including:

- **Merchant Name** – used to personalize phone call responses (e.g., greeting callers with the restaurant or business name).
- **Merchant ID** – used to identify and connect orders and inventory with the correct merchant.
- **Merchant Email** – used to send order notifications and relevant service communications.

This information is accessed via the platform's API using secure, authenticated access provided during the merchant's app installation or connection process. The specific data available may vary depending on the integrated platform.

### 1.2 Inventory Data (from Integrated Platforms)

To enable the AI agent to accurately present and suggest menu items or services during customer calls, we read the merchant's inventory information from their integrated platform, which may include:

- **Items** (names, prices, availability)
- **Modifiers** (e.g., extra cheese, spicy level, size options)
- **Options** and **Categories**
- **Product descriptions** and **availability status**

This data is synchronized with PolyDial and used to generate conversational responses to customers, ensuring orders reflect the merchant's current offerings. The specific inventory data available depends on what the integrated platform provides.

### 1.3 Order Data

PolyDial interacts with orders from your integrated platform in the following ways:

- **Creating Orders:** When an AI agent takes an order from a customer, PolyDial creates the order in its internal system and then submits it to the merchant's integrated platform account to ensure proper fulfillment and processing.
- **Reading Orders:** PolyDial reads order data from your integrated platform to verify fulfillment, track status, and support merchant analytics.

This ensures a synchronized, reliable ordering flow between PolyDial and your business management system.

### 1.4 End Customer Information

When a customer calls a merchant's PolyDial number:

- The AI agent may ask for the customer's name, which is stored along with the caller's phone number in PolyDial's database.
- This data is used to personalize future calls (e.g., greeting the customer by name), and for analytics, loyalty, and CRM features for the merchant.

PolyDial does not share customer information with other merchants or third parties without consent.

### 1.5 Call Transcripts and Metadata

PolyDial processes customer phone calls through AI technology. During these interactions:

- **Text Transcripts:** We convert speech to text in real-time and store text transcriptions of conversations. Audio recordings are not retained.
- **Call Metadata:** We collect information associated with calls, including date, time, duration, and caller phone number.

**Purpose:** Call transcripts and metadata are used to process customer orders, improve AI performance, provide merchant analytics, and support customer service functions.

- Complying with all applicable laws regarding customer communications and data processing in your jurisdiction
- Providing any required notices to your customers about how their information will be processed
- Obtaining any necessary consents as required by applicable law
- Establishing and communicating your own privacy practices to your customers

You represent and warrant that your use of our Services complies with all applicable laws, and you agree to indemnify and hold PolyDial and IdeationMax Inc. harmless from any claims, damages, or liabilities arising from your failure to comply with such laws.

### 1.6 Automatically Collected Information

We automatically collect certain technical information when you use our Services:

- **Device Information:** Device type, operating system, and browser type
- **Connection Information:** IP address and network information
- **Usage Data:** Information about how you use our platform, including features accessed, actions taken, and dashboard activity

This information is used to ensure proper service delivery, improve our platform, and provide analytics.

### 1.7 Payment Information

**We do not collect, process, or store any payment card information or other payment details.**

Payment processing varies based on your integration:

- **Clover Integration:** For merchants integrating with Clover, all PolyDial subscription fees and charges are processed entirely by Clover through their payment platform. Your payment information is provided to and stored by Clover, not PolyDial. Your payment relationship is governed by your agreement with Clover.
- **Square Integration:** For merchants integrating with Square, PolyDial subscription fees are processed through our secure third-party payment processor. We do not have access to your payment card details.
- **Toast Integration:** For merchants integrating with Toast, PolyDial subscription fees are processed through our secure third-party payment processor. We do not have access to your payment card details.
- **Other Integrations or Standalone Service:** For merchants using other platforms or standalone PolyDial services, payments are processed by our secure third-party payment processors. These processors collect and store your payment information directly; PolyDial does not have access to your payment card details.

## 2. How We Use Information

We use the information collected for the following purposes:

- To provide and operate the PolyDial service
- To personalize customer interactions on behalf of merchants
- To process and synchronize orders between PolyDial and your integrated platform
- To create and maintain call transcripts for order processing, quality assurance, and service improvement
- To send merchant notifications about new orders or account activity
- To analyze customer behavior for loyalty programs and merchant insights
- To improve service reliability, AI accuracy, and features
- To comply with legal and regulatory obligations

## 3. How We Share Information

We **do not sell** personal data of merchants or customers. We may share collected information as follows:

- **With Integrated Platforms:** Orders created through PolyDial are pushed to your integrated platform (such as Clover, Square, or Toast). Merchant and inventory data flows between your platform and PolyDial as part of the service. For Clover-integrated merchants, Clover also processes all payment transactions on our behalf.
- **Service Providers:** We share information with third-party vendors who perform services on our behalf, including:
  - Cloud hosting providers (for call transcripts and data storage)
  - Analytics providers
  - Communication infrastructure providers
  - Payment processors (for non-Clover integrations)

  These providers are bound by strict confidentiality and security obligations, and we do not have access to payment card information.
- **Legal or Regulatory Requests:** If required by law, we may disclose information to comply with legal obligations or enforce agreements.
- **Business Transfers:** In case of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of the transaction. Users will be notified of any such transfer, and the acquiring entity will be bound by this Privacy Policy.

## 4. Cookies and Tracking

Our platform uses cookies and similar technologies primarily for:

- **Essential Functions:** Session management and authentication to keep you logged into your dashboard
- **Analytics:** Understanding how our Services are used to improve functionality

We do not use cookies for targeted advertising. Merchants can adjust their browser settings to manage cookie preferences.

## 5. Data Retention

- Merchant and inventory data is retained as long as the merchant account is active.
- Order data is retained in line with merchant needs and legal obligations.
- Call transcripts are retained to support order fulfillment, merchant analytics, and service improvement. Merchants can request deletion of specific transcripts.
- Customer name and phone number are retained to support personalization and loyalty features; merchants can request deletion.

## 6. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect collected information, including:

- **Encryption:** Data is encrypted both in transit and at rest, including call transcripts and customer information
- **Access Controls:** Restricted access to personal information on a need-to-know basis
- **Secure Infrastructure:** Use of certified, secure data centers
- **Regular Security Audits:** Ongoing assessment of security measures
- **Incident Response:** Procedures for detecting and responding to security incidents

While no system is completely secure, we take reasonable steps to prevent unauthorized access, use, or disclosure.

## 7. International Data Transfers

PolyDial may process data in the United States or Canada. For Canadian merchants and customers, this may mean that information is subject to U.S. laws and accessible to U.S. authorities under lawful orders. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for international data transfers in compliance with applicable laws.
